# ScribaSheet — User Guide

## 1. Create an Account & Project
Register or sign in, then click **New Transcription** on your Dashboard. Choose a title, primary instrument (e.g., Piano, Acoustic Guitar, Vocals), and transcription mode.

## 2. Upload or Record Audio
In the **Audio / Video Input** panel on the left:
- Drag and drop an MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, AAC, MP4, MOV, or WEBM file (up to 100 MB), **or**
- Click **Record Microphone** to capture a live performance directly in your browser.

## 3. Configure & Transcribe
Select your transcription mode, tempo (auto-detect or manual BPM), quantization grid (e.g., 1/16 note), and optional advanced pitch/sensitivity settings. Click **Start AI Transcription** and monitor live progress.

## 4. Review, Edit & Compare
- **Sheet Music:** Inspect the rendered MusicXML score and zoom in/out.
- **Piano Roll Editor:** Double-click to add notes, drag notes to adjust pitch/timing, drag right edges to resize duration, adjust velocity, or use Undo/Redo.
- **A/B Playback:** Use the top waveform player to toggle between **Original Audio** and **Transcribed MIDI**, adjust speed, loop, or enable the metronome.
- **Chords & Guitar Tab:** Review and edit detected chord symbols and standard-tuning guitar string/fret assignments.

## 5. Save & Export
Edits autosave automatically (or click **Save Score**). Open the **Export Center** tab to download `.mid`, `.musicxml`, `.pdf` sheet music, or `.wav` audio.
